# Halcyon content drafts

Review page for Halcyon Trader Funding's social content, prepared by Miren Partners.

Open `index.html` (or the GitHub Pages link) to see every draft grouped by content pillar:

- Community & Proof: founder talking heads, payout calls with traders, plus reference formats (tier list, profitable or not, reacting to clips and news)
- Entertainment: AI character tutorials, memes, recreated memes and ads
- Education: explainers and Trade of the Week
- Promo: the Halcyon Dynamic promo and four motion options

All drafts are for review. Prices, rules and claims are checked with Halcyon before anything is posted. Reference videos belong to their creators and are shown for comparison only.

## Folders

- `media/` videos and images used by the page
- `source/` how the page and the motion pieces were built
  - `build_site.py` builds `index.html`
  - `motion/` HTML motion pages (rendered with Playwright) and Trade of the Week sources
  - `research/` notes taken from halcyontraderfunding.com
