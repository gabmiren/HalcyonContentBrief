## Rules from the legal pages that matter for social

All legal pages say "last updated November 24, 2024", even though Arena launched in Oct 2026.

- **Eligibility:** 18+ (Trader Agreement, Privacy Policy). KYC through Veriff within 5 business days when asked. No restricted countries listed on the site (they are in the onboarding form).
- **Banned trading practices** (rewards can be denied, days deleted, accounts closed or banned): more than 250 contracts a day, fully automated trading or bots without written permission, news straddles around releases, hedging (including across accounts and micro vs mini), copy or group trading, trading someone else's account, "windfall" days (80 to 100% of profit from one trade or day), no stop loss, repeated resets, more than one email per customer.
- **Refunds:** every sale is final, "NON REFUNDABLE with no exceptions". A chargeback means every account closed, profits forfeited and a permanent ban.
- **Terms in the legal pages:** "Sim accounts", "Funded Accounts", "reward-based accounts", "Simulated Reward Policy". The T&C never uses "reward account".
- **Disclaimer lines used across the site:** "Futures trading contains substantial risk and is not for every investor." · "At no time do you risk your own capital." · "There are no promises of rewards or returns." · "not an investment opportunity" · "most traders do not succeed" · CFTC Rule 4.41 hypothetical-results notice · testimonials "may not be representative".
- **Testimonials and likeness:** no clause gives Halcyon the right to use a trader's name or face. Get a signed release for every proof post.
- **Non-disparagement:** traders agree not to disparage the company. Never cite this publicly to shut down a complaint.
- **Anti-fraud:** the official domain is www.halcyontraderfunding.com; fake sites copy the branding.

## Site copy our content should NOT repeat

- "Instant funding" (in every page's metadata and the giveaway copy). Halcyon is evaluation-first.
- "Funded traders" / "Funded required" (on /start and the Arena pages). Use "reward account".
- "Trade today. Get paid today." / "same-day rewards" / "Quick payouts" unless Halcyon confirms in writing.
- The "$82 to $572, ~600%" example and other single certificates presented as typical.
- "Someone is getting paid. Is it gonna be you?", "You won't win unless you join", "Win big": FOMO and win framing on a paid-pool competition.
- "Cleanest payout flow in the industry", "thousands of traders are switching": unsubstantiated superlatives.
- "At no time do you risk your own capital": traders do pay fees, so say the fee is the only cost instead.
